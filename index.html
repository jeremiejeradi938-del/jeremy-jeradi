Who is Jeremy Jeradi

A minister devoted to the presence of God

Jeremy Jeradi is a Kenyan minister of the Gospel, preacher, and revivalist — leading worship gatherings, revival meetings, and conferences where he preaches the Word and ministers through prayer and the Holy Spirit. Through Great Nation Community, he leads discipleship and leadership development, advancing worship, prayer, and community transformation. He is also a songwriter and gospel recording artist, carrying the same message he preaches into song, having released songs of blessing and worship such as Nimekutana Na Wewe, Sijasahau, and many more.
